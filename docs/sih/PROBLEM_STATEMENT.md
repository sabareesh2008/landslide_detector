# SIH Problem Statement — Disaster Management (Early Warning)

## 1. Context & Geographic Setting
The **Rangpo–Singtam corridor along National Highway 10 (NH-10)** is the sole arterial lifeline connecting the state of Sikkim and the strategic border regions of Kalimpong/Lada to mainland India.
- **Geological Fragility**: Traverses the active Main Boundary Thrust (MBT) and Main Central Thrust (MCT) with intensely weathered Daling Group phyllites, quartzites, and chlorite schists.
- **Monsoonal Triggers**: Extreme orographic precipitation (often exceeding 150mm/24h) causes recurring catastrophic slope failures, debris flows, and Teesta river bank erosion.
- **Human & Economic Cost**: Annually causes loss of lives, strandings of thousands of tourists/military convoys, and economic disruption costing hundreds of crores of rupees.

---

## 2. Fundamental Weaknesses of Existing Systems
1. **Static Hazard Mapping**: Traditional macro-zonation maps provide static susceptibility but cannot predict time-varying risk during dynamic monsoonal storms.
2. **Disconnected Point Telemetry**: Rain gauges and inclinometers operate in silos without coupling to numerical GIS and predictive slope stability models.
3. **Simulated/Uncalibrated AI Models**: Many academic prototypes report unverified 99% accuracies on synthetic data without real ground truth calibration or physical explainability.
4. **Actionability Gap**: When an alert is triggered, emergency responders lack automated route blockage modeling, designated shelter capacities, and machinery pre-positioning guidance.
5. **Language & Connectivity Barriers**: Alerts issued in English fail to reach rural truck drivers and mountain communities when cellular towers collapse during cloudbursts.

---

## 3. Project Objective
Deliver **Landslide Sentinel AI**, an end-to-end early warning and disaster response system combining **calibrated machine learning**, **10x10 spatial GIS matrices**, **IoT ground telemetry**, **multimodal field evidence**, and **offline-first multilingual emergency dispatch** for the NH-10 Sikkim corridor.
