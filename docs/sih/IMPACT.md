# SIH Social, Economic & Strategic Impact

## 1. Life Safety & Zero-Casualty Goal
- **Lead-Time Early Warning**: Provides 12 to 24 hours of advance probabilistic lead-time before monsoonal slope collapse, allowing preventative traffic regulation.
- **Vulnerable Toe Evacuation**: Identifies settlements within high run-out zones (e.g., Majhitar riverside, Bardang labor camps) for safe evacuation to designated highland ridge shelters.

---

## 2. Economic & Infrastructure Protection
- **NH-10 Lifeline Continuity**: Reduces average highway blockage duration from 48-72 hours down to 6-12 hours through predictive pre-positioning of heavy excavators and loaders.
- **Logistics & Tourism Protection**: Sikkim relies on NH-10 for 100% of its petroleum, vegetables, and medicine supply. Preventing unorganized strandings protects perishable goods and prevents tourist stranding in landslide chutes.

---

## 3. National Security & Strategic Defense
- **Border Road Corridor**: NH-10 is the primary logistics artery for Indian Armed Forces moving towards Nathu La, Doklam, and North Sikkim borders.
- **Mission Readiness**: Equips the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) and Project Swastik with real-time sector-level geotechnical risk intelligence.

---

## 4. Alignment with National Disaster Management Frameworks
- **NDMA & SDMA Integration**: Fully compatible with State Emergency Operation Centre (SEOC) alert escalation workflows.
- **Sendai Framework Target G**: Direct contribution to substantially increasing the availability of and access to multi-hazard early warning systems and disaster risk information.
