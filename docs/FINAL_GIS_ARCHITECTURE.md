# Landslide Sentinel AI — Final GIS & Spatial Risk Architecture

## 1. Spatial Domain & Geographic Bounds
- **Target Corridor**: Rangpo–Singtam Sector along NH-10 (Sikkim, India).
- **Bounding Box**:
  - Minimum Latitude: `27.14°N`, Maximum Latitude: `27.25°N`
  - Minimum Longitude: `88.46°E`, Maximum Longitude: `88.54°E`
- **Spatial Grid**: 100 uniform cells ($10 \times 10$ matrix), representing approximately $1.1\text{ km} \times 0.8\text{ km}$ spatial resolution per cell.

---

## 2. GeoJSON Data Layers

1. **`data/gis/nh10_road_network.geojson`**:
   - 4 segmented highway lines from Rangpo Border Checkpost (Km 0.0) to Singtam Bazar Bridge (Km 12.8).
   - Properties: `segment_id`, `name`, `length_km`, `criticality`, `chronic_slide_zone`.
2. **`data/gis/corridor_infrastructure.geojson`**:
   - 12 critical point assets (Singtam District Hospital, Rangpo Bridge, Teesta Stage V powerhouse, culverts, schools).
3. **`data/gis/settlements.geojson`**:
   - 5 major population centers (Rangpo, Singtam, Majhitar, Bardang, Mining).
4. **`data/gis/rivers.geojson`**:
   - River centerlines for Teesta River and Rangpo Chu.
5. **`data/gis/risk_zones.geojson`**:
   - 100 polygon cells generated by `scripts/gis/spatial_processor.py` with embedded slope, aspect, real-time rainfall, and calibrated risk probabilities.
6. **`data/gis/hotspots.json`**:
   - Top 6 ranked vulnerable hotspots (Bardang Curve, Majhitar Hill, 20th Mile, Mining Cut, etc.).

## 3. Leaflet GIS Visual Styling & Accessibility
- **Non-Color-Only Universal Symbols**:
  - `CRITICAL`: 🔴 Solid Red with `[!]` badge.
  - `HIGH`: 🟠 Orange with `[▲]` badge.
  - `WATCH`: 🟡 Amber with `[◆]` badge.
  - `LOW`: 🟢 Green with `[✓]` badge.
- **Interactive Drill-Down**: Clicking any cell, bridge, or highway segment opens a responsive modal showing terrain slope, rainfall depth, explainability factors, and designated shelter evacuation paths.
